Welcome to my site. This is where I'll share research notes, short tutorials, and
updates on what I'm working on in seismology — earthquake detection, relocation, and
machine-learning phase picking.

### Why a blog?

Writing things down forces clarity. I plan to use these notes to:

- explain methods I find useful (and the gotchas I hit),
- document small tools and workflows, and
- keep a running log of project progress.

### How to add a post

Create a file in `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md` with front matter like the top
of this file, then write in Markdown. Math works too, via MathJax:

$$
t_S - t_P \approx \frac{r}{v_P}\left(\frac{v_P}{v_S} - 1\right)
$$

That's it — thanks for reading.
